How it works

  1. Tap to cancelWhen the surf says no, one tap cancels the session. Every booking on it goes straight onto a "to rebook" list.
  2. Tap to rebookEach booking shows the next three sessions with enough free places for that whole group. Tap one and it's moved.
  3. See what's at riskA money screen shows how many bookings still wait and their value in euro. Anything not rebooked after 48 hours turns red.

Big buttons, bottom-of-screen tabs and an Undo button after every tap, so it works with one wet hand.
