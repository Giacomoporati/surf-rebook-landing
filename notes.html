1. Niche

Who they areSmall, owner-run surf schools on Ireland's west coast (Clare, Sligo, Donegal, Kerry): one beach, a handful of instructors, busy in summer and often cancelled by wind and swell.
BuyerThe owner of the surf school.
Daily userThe owner or head instructor, on the beach, on their phone.
Pain (invented quotes)"When the surf's off I lose the whole morning on the phone with the diary on my knee." / "Some bookings never get rebooked; they just slip between WhatsApp and the diary."

I narrowed the case market (outdoor activity providers in Ireland whose sessions get cancelled by weather) from a wider list I considered (surf, scuba diving, sailing, kayaking, adventure groups) down to surf schools, because the alpha was built for a surf school and the page needs to speak to one group.

2. Technology and how it's done today

Type of technology: a mobile-first cancellation and rebooking tool, a small operations web app for the weather-cancelled day. It sits beside whatever the school already uses to take bookings.

How the niche does it today: a paper diary or spreadsheet, then phone calls and WhatsApp messages one customer at a time, or cancelling and rescheduling bookings one by one inside a general booking system.

Two real products that already do part of it

FareHarbor, fareharbor.com (checked 20 October 2026)
An all-in-one booking and business management platform for tour and activity operators. It covers online sales and operations, and is aimed mainly at established, higher-volume operators.
Bókun, bokun.io/activity-booking-software (checked 20 October 2026)
A booking platform for tour and activity businesses. Operators can close time slots and customers can change or cancel their own bookings through a self-service portal.

Gap: both are full booking platforms. Washout does one job, getting every washed-out booking rebooked fast from a phone, for small schools that may not want a whole new booking system.

3. Business model (assumption)

€99 one-off set-up plus €29 per month. Reasoning: one family of four that rebooks instead of being lost is worth €180 (4 × €45), which covers six months of the fee.

4. Lean PRD

HypothesisOwners of small west-coast surf schools will join a waiting list for a phone tool that rebooks weather-cancelled lessons.
AudienceOwners of small surf schools on Ireland's west coast.
Call to actionJoin the waiting list.
Success measure20 waiting-list sign-ups, and at least 1 in 10 visitors opening the demo, within 4 weeks of sharing the page with surf schools (target, assumption).
Out of scopePayments and refunds, automatic customer messages, importing real bookings, multi-staff sync, linking to existing booking systems.

5. Alpha test

What I tried: on my phone, one-handed, the way Niamh would use it on the beach: cancelled the 16:30 session in Today, checked its booking moved to Rebook, moved a booking to a suggested session with one tap, refreshed the page to check the changes were saved, and checked the totals on Money.

What broke or confused me: the Money screen said "25 persons waiting", which reads wrong and looks unfinished.

What I changed: asked the AI to fix the wording to "people", then republished the demo and checked it on my phone again.

Assumptions

  • The app runs in one browser on Niamh's phone, so the data saves on that device only.
  • Six fixed session times a day, 12 places each, €45 per person, as in the case.
  • The 48-hour red flag counts from the moment the session was cancelled.
  • Sessions that have already started cannot be cancelled. Niamh can also cancel future days.
  • An Undo button appears after every tap, because one-tap cancelling with wet hands could cause mistakes.
